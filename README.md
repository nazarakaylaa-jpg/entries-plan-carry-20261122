# Fiverr AI Photo Gigs

A 275-character plan an AI agent can carry out itself to turn $100 into the most money in 30 days, for make.fun's "The plan make.fun will carry out in public" bounty.

The plan: on day one, list three $15 AI photo gigs on Fiverr (product cleanup, Etsy images, property photos) with 1-hour delivery no human matches, spend $30 promoting them for five 5-star reviews, and let Fiverr search compound. Most likely outcome: 25 orders by day 30, about $300 after Fiverr's 20% fee.

## Run

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) writes the plan to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output node main.ts
```
